## Abstract

Adaptive skins are still scored by energy and comfort scalars that do not record whether the exterior encodes interior state. A single Pareto front is treated as the complete design set. Temporal Facade Entropy is TFE(i, t) = H_space(t) × H_time(i), the product of spatial and temporal Shannon entropies, range [0, log2(N) × log2(T)]. TFE = 0 if and only if the envelope is spatially uniform and temporally frozen. The predicted ordering TFE_optimised > TFE_rule-based > TFE_static = 0 is a falsification condition, not a result.

Multi-objective facade search is rewritten as an architectural Ising Hamiltonian. Genes for depth, aperture, rotation, hysteresis ε, maximum step Δs, and radiation weight wr are normalised to σ ∈ [−1, +1]^6. The energy is H_facade(σ) = −∑_{i<j} J^arch_ij · σ_i · σ_j − ∑_i h_i · σ_i + λ·P(σ), with J^arch from the four-objective Jacobian. Pairwise signs on (ε, Δs, wr) form a frustrated triangle (Φ_ijk = −1), predicting multiple local minima and a replica-symmetry-breaking (RSB) signature in P_arch(d). Detection uses Wallacei runs with distinct seeds and a Hartigan dip test; RSB-equivalent only if p < 0.05 and K ≥ 2. The protocol is unexecuted; RSB is not confirmed.

TFE > 0 without operational power is proposed via a spatial gradient of Bacillus subtilis spore monolayers on cork. Maximum angle θ_max(i) = f(n_i) ≈ α · n_i pending calibration; relaxation θ(i, t) = θ_max(i) · (1 − exp(−t/τ)), τ ≈ 180 s. Differential n_i forces H_space(t) > 0 off the humidity reference; fabrication is not claimed. BIR(t) = H_boundary(t) / H_interior(t), H_boundary(t) = H_space(t), is capped on [0, 1]. BIR is a macroscale holographic analogy citing S_BH = A / (4 · l_p²) as a reference bound, not an AdS/CFT derivation. Layer gradients generate TFE; TFE over interior entropy is BIR; H_facade predicts the high-BIR family.
