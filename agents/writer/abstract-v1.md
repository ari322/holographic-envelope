# Holographic Envelopes: A Spin-Glass Information Framework for Adaptive Architectural Skins

**Target journal:** Building and Environment (Elsevier)
**Word count:** 258

## Abstract

Building envelopes remain static or only pseudo-adaptive. They execute energy-driven commands but do not encode interior state as measurable information, and a single parametric search is taken to exhaust the Pareto landscape. This paper states an information-theoretic and statistical-physics framework for adaptive architectural skins. Temporal Facade Entropy is TFE(i, t) = H_space(t) × H_time(i), the product of Shannon entropy of panel-state occupancy at time t and of panel i over time. TFE ranges over [0, log2(N) × log2(T)]; TFE = 0 is a spatially and temporally uniform envelope. Multi-objective facade design is an architectural Ising Hamiltonian H_facade(σ) = −∑_{i<j} J^arch_ij · σ_i · σ_j − ∑_i h_i · σ_i + λ·P(σ). The coupling matrix J^arch contains a frustrated triangle on (ε, Δs, wr), predicting a multi-modal landscape and replica-symmetry-breaking (RSB) structure in the pairwise gene-distance distribution P_arch(d). RSB is a protocol-level prediction and is not confirmed. The material route is Bacillus subtilis spore monolayers on cork, with a spatial gradient of layer counts n_i such that θ_max(i) = f(n_i) and θ(i, t) = θ_max(i) · (1 − exp(−t/τ)) with τ ≈ 180 s. Differential n_i yields H_space(t) > 0, hence TFE > 0, at zero operational energy. Boundary Information Ratio is BIR(t) = H_boundary(t) / H_interior(t), with H_boundary(t) = H_space(t) and BIR capped on [0, 1]. BIR is a macroscale holographic analogy that uses S_BH = A / (4 · l_p²) only as a reference bound; it is not derived from AdS/CFT. Empirical tests of the Wallacei RSB protocol, cork θ_max(n), and BIR on building sensors remain open.
