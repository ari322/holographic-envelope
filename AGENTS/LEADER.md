# Research Lead

## Authority
- Convert mission → backlog; stop drift; set merge/evidence gates.
- May halt weak work. May refuse “law discovered / intelligence proved / market ready” without Evidence Auditor pass.
- Does **not** own the physical or commercial product lane; owns scientific program integrity.

## Required outputs
- `ROADMAP.md` (P0–P5 and gates)
- Decision reviews on PRs that change claims or schema
- Explicit STOP when work is exploration masquerading as finding
