# Prompts — Visual Generation

> For Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion, Firefly.
> Each prompt targets a specific concept from the project.

---

## V01 — Phase Transition Pavilion

```
An architectural pavilion whose structure undergoes a visible phase transition:
the left half is a chaotic, fluid, branching lattice of glowing energy nodes (SYK quantum chaos phase),
the right half crystallises into a rigid, ordered topological spin-glass geometry with hexagonal cells.
The boundary between phases is a blurred gradient zone.
Highly detailed structural physics visualisation, biomimetic quantum metamaterials,
ultra-realistic architectural render, cinematic side lighting, deep blue and amber palette.
--ar 16:9 --style raw
```

## V02 — Bacterial Spore Facade

```
A close-up macro photograph of an architectural facade panel system:
each rectangular panel is made of pale cork with a visible gradient of biological texture,
some panels are flat (zero humidity), others curl at different angles (high humidity),
creating a wave-like spatial pattern across the facade without any visible electronics or actuators.
Morning light, soft shadows, warm natural tones, hyper-realistic material photography.
--ar 3:2 --style raw
```

## V03 — Holographic Event Horizon Building

```
An aerial architectural render of a building whose facade is visualised as an event horizon:
the exterior surface glows with encoded data patterns (heat maps, occupancy density contours,
thermal gradients) that represent the interior activity of the building.
The interior is dark and complex; the facade is luminous and information-rich.
Black, white, and deep blue palette, futuristic, physically-based rendering.
--ar 16:9 --style raw
```

## V04 — Spin-Glass Energy Landscape as Architecture

```
A three-dimensional visualisation of a rugged energy landscape (multiple valleys and peaks)
overlaid with an architectural floor plan: each valley in the landscape corresponds to
a different facade design family, the peaks are energy barriers between design families.
The highest peak is labelled 'the illusion of a single optimal design'.
Scientific visualisation aesthetic, topographic contours, glowing Pareto solution points.
--ar 16:9
```

## V05 — BIR = 1 Building (Holographic Limit)

```
A photorealistic architectural render of a building at dusk:

and temperature — the building skin glows differently where people are concentrated inside.

Soft amber interior light bleeding through, cinematic atmosphere, high detail.
```
