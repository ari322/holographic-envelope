# Research Prompts

## Theoretical
Act as a scientific architectural researcher. Build a bounded research framework for an adaptive envelope project that uses the holographic principle only as an information analogy, phase transition as a state logic model, and spin-glass frustration as a multi-objective optimization analogy. Separate physically validated claims from conceptual transfers and define a falsifiable research agenda.

## Design computation
Act as a computational architect and Grasshopper systems designer. Create a parametric facade workflow that maps radiation, occupancy, temperature, and safety constraints into panel states. Include data trees, hysteresis, state transitions, and validation metrics. Avoid speculative physics claims and keep all outputs testable.

## Material study
Act as a building technology researcher. Compare PCM, SMA, SMP, hygromorphic systems, and mechanical metamaterials for adaptive envelopes. For each, evaluate trigger, response, durability, scale, fabrication, fire risk, maintenance, and suitability for a 1:5 prototype.
