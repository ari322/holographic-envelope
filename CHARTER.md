# Intelligent Superstructures Research Program

**Holographic Envelope** = first experimental body and repository name.  
**Intelligent Superstructures** = strategic mission name.  
**Octopus** = one computational-scale exemplar — **not** the manager or owner of this repository.

Owner lock: 2026-09-23 (AEST). Season goal: build a **discovery machine** for shared organizing laws — not declare a mega-law, not sell a “smart panel.”

## Mission question

> What substrate- and scale-independent organizing laws let local parts become an adaptive, memory-bearing, feedback-driven, self-organizing structure?

Every new work item must attach to a falsifiable hypothesis, a model, an experiment, or a cross-scale comparison. Proving “smart architecture” is the aim; shipping a panel is downstream.

## Intelligence gate

Motion alone is not intelligence. A claim of “intelligent structure” must show **at least three of five** measurable properties:

| Property | Test question |
|---|---|
| Sensing | Does an environmental input correlate repeatably with structure state? |
| Memory | Does input history change the present output? |
| Feedback | Does prior structure state affect the next response? |
| Self-organization | Does a local rule produce collective pattern beyond the sum of units? |
| Adaptation | Is performance preserved or recovered under changing conditions? |

Purely kinetic or interactive demos that fail this gate stay labeled **interactive / kinetic**, not intelligent.

## Research architecture (four pillars)

| Pillar | Question | Valid outputs |
|---|---|---|
| Material Intelligence | How does matter sense, remember, or change state? | response curves, hysteresis, degradation, uncertainty |
| Spatial Intelligence | How do many units produce collective behavior? | arrays, local rules, global patterns, homogeneous controls |
| Boundary Intelligence | How does the boundary mediate inside vs outside? | interior state ↔ boundary morphology relations |
| Cross-Scale Intelligence | Which principles recur across matter, life, and computation? | comparative matrix + mechanism mapping |

## Claim discipline

- Inspiration ≠ formal model ≠ tested analogy ≠ validated result — label every claim (`claim_type` in idea schema).
- No agent may alone declare: “law discovered,” “intelligence proved,” or “market-ready.” Claims are **evidence-gated**.
- Ideas that cannot fill `schemas/idea.yaml` are **exploration** only — not core findings.
