# Material Families

## Candidate classes

### Phase change materials (PCM)
- Main value: latent heat storage and thermal buffering
- Strongest use: indoor thermal moderation
- Weakness: does not itself produce expressive geometric movement

### Shape memory alloys (SMA)
- Main value: repeatable heat-triggered shape recovery
- Strongest use: small adaptive components and shutters
- Weakness: fatigue, cost, thermal control complexity

### Shape memory polymers (SMP)
- Main value: lightweight programmable shape change
- Strongest use: small panels and experimental deployable skins
- Weakness: durability, fire performance, scale limitations

### Hygromorphic systems
- Main value: passive humidity-responsive movement
- Strongest use: no-motor demonstrators and bio-inspired envelopes
- Weakness: slow response and environmental durability challenges

### Mechanical metamaterials
- Main value: geometry-driven control of stiffness, deformation, and energy absorption
- Strongest use: deployable lattices, auxetic panels, controlled deformation
- Weakness: fabrication complexity and joint design

## Selection logic

Material choice should follow target function:
- thermal regulation,
- aperture movement,
- shading response,
- acoustic filtering,
- deployability,
- symbolic legibility.

The project should avoid selecting materials based on visual futurism alone.
